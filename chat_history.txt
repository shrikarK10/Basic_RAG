Q: What is a ANN
A: Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) can be represented as a weighted directed graph where artificial neurons are the nodes and the connections between neuron outputs and inputs are directed edges with weights. ANNs receive input signals from external sources as patterns or images in the form of vectors, denoted as x(n) for each input. These inputs are then multiplied by their corresponding weights, which represent the strength of the interconnection between neurons and are crucial for the ANN to solve a specific problem.
----------------------------------------
Q: Different layers in ANN
A: The different layers in an Artificial Neural Network (ANN) are:

*   **Input Layer:** Accepts inputs in various formats.
*   **Hidden Layer:** Located between the input and output layers, it performs calculations to find hidden features and patterns.
*   **Output Layer:** Conveys the final output after the input goes through transformations in the hidden layer.
