Q: Explain k-means algorithm
A: The K-means clustering algorithm uses exemplars to create clusters of similar data points. Exemplars can be either centroids, which find a center of mass based on a chosen distance metric, or medoids, which find the most centrally located data point. The most commonly used centroid is the arithmetic mean, which minimizes the squared Euclidean distance to all other points.
